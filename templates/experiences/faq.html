Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions regarding device optimization and sync.

How does device telemetry help improve performance?

Telemetry allows the client engine to tune memory allocation, frame rates, and render fidelity based on hardware concurrency and battery status.

What permissions are requested and why?

Optional permissions like camera and location are requested to provide interactive live features. You can deny any request, and all content will continue working.

Is my private data protected?

Yes, sessions operate under standard browser sandbox controls. No unauthorized gallery scans or background access occurs without permission.
